TITLE
Detect It, Then Write the Rule
A study guide that pairs attack patterns with the logs, filters, and IDS rules that detect them.
ACT 1: SETUP
Setting Role
You're preparing for a senior network analyst test covering attacks and detection. You're the analyst who must recognize an attack pattern and write the detection.
Point A (Where am I?) Call to Action (How do I get from A to B?) Point B (Where do I want to be?)
Attack types, log sources, and rule syntax are scattered, and hard to recall together. Work section by section, practice with samples, and write the rule from memory. Test-ready skills in detection and rule writing.
Transition 1The guide organizes the material by test section. Three ideas explain it: the attacks, the detection toolkit, and the patterns behind future signatures.
